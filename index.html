    Part C: time for the officer to catch the car

    The officer's chase has two phases. Both end at the same place: 1.40 × 10³ m from where the car passed.

    Phase 1, speeding up. Time to reach 30.6 m/s, and the distance covered doing it:

    t₁ = v / a = 30.6 / 4.20 = 7.29 s

    d₁ = v² / 2a = (30.6)² / (2 × 4.20) = 111 m

    Phase 2, constant speed. The rest of the 1400 m is covered at 30.6 m/s:

    t₂ = (1400 − 111.5) / 30.6 = 1288.5 / 30.6 = 42.1 s

    Total pursuit time.

    t = t₁ + t₂ = 7.29 + 42.11 = 49.4 s

    The officer catches the car 49.4 s after taking off. That's 51.4 s after the car passed the officer, which also means the car was doing 1400 / 51.4 ≈ 27.2 m/s in the breakdown lane.
